# Public journey override

Inherits `../MASTER.md`.

- Tone: reassuring and plain-language. Avoid internal queue terminology until the lifecycle explanation introduces it.
- Landing hero uses a 7/5 desktop split, a prominent Book a Visit action, Track My Queue, and practical arrival instructions.
- Public content width is 1200px; the representative tracker is 960px; login is 1040px with a branded side panel at desktop.
- Booking explains that the appointment is Scheduled and that a number is issued only after physical check-in.
- Private tracking prioritizes lifecycle status, queue number, people ahead, estimated wait, and next instruction. Supporting reference and QR follow. Private-link access requirements stay intact.
- Display mode increases contrast and queue-number scale, removes decorative chrome, and never exposes names or phone numbers.
- On mobile, actions stack, queue information stays before the QR section, and the three arrival steps become a vertical sequence.
- A valid zero-minute estimate remains zero. Missing or invalid estimates say temporarily unavailable. Failed updates keep the last valid information and its successful update time.
