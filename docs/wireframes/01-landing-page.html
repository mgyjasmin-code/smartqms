How It Works

Simple Steps to Join the Queue

Two ways to use the system — online or walk-in. Both are quick and hassle-free.

1
Fill Out Information
Enter your first name, last name, phone number, and select your health service. No account required.
2
Get Your Ticket
Receive a QR code ticket with your reference number and appointment details. Print or save it.
3
Arrive & Check-In
Show your QR code at the health center. Staff will scan it to activate your queue ticket.
4
Track & Be Called
Monitor your position using your reference number. You'll be called when it's your turn.
AI-Powered Intelligence

Smarter Queuing with Machine Learning

Our system uses a Random Forest model trained on historical visit data to predict waiting times and optimize service flow.

Real-Time Wait Prediction
ML model predicts your estimated wait time based on queue depth, service type, and historical patterns.
FIFO Queue Algorithm
First-In, First-Out ensures fair and transparent queue management for all patients.
Grace Period Management
Auto-void missed calling tickets after a configurable grace period (3–5 mins), freeing up counters efficiently.
Public Queue Display
Real-time display board for waiting room showing current serving numbers and next in queue.
