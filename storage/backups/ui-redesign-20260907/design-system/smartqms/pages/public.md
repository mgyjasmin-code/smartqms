# Public journey override

Inherits `../MASTER.md`.

- Tone: reassuring and plain-language. Avoid internal queue terminology until the lifecycle explanation introduces it.
- Landing hero uses a 7/5 desktop split, one prominent Pre-register Visit action, and one secondary status action.
- Public content width is 1200px; form/tracker content is 920px; login is 1040px with a branded side panel at desktop.
- Booking explains that the appointment is Scheduled and that a number is issued only after physical check-in.
- Tracker prioritizes lifecycle status, QR, reference, service, and next instruction. PII never appears in reference-only lookup.
- Display mode increases contrast and queue-number scale, removes decorative chrome, and never exposes names or phone numbers.
- On mobile, actions stack, QR stays at least 180px, and lifecycle steps become a vertical timeline.
